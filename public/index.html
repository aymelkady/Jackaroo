How this Jackaroo plays

Teams of two, partners sit opposite. Each player has 4 marbles. Bring all 8 marbles of your team into your safe zones to win. You must play a card if any card in your hand has a legal move; otherwise you burn a card.

ATake a marble out to your base, or move 1 or 11.
KTake a marble out, or move 13 killing every marble you pass (option).
QMove 12.
JSwap one of your marbles with any other player's marble on the track.
10 9 8 6 3 2Move that many steps.
7Move 7, or split the 7 between two of your marbles.
5Move any marble on the track 5 steps, yours or anyone's (option).
4Move 4 steps backwards.
JokerPlays as any card (option).
  • Landing on a marble sends it home. You cannot land on your own marble.
  • A marble on its own base is protected: nobody can pass it, kill it or swap it, not even a King.
  • Taking a marble out kills whatever is sitting on your base.
  • You enter your safe zone after a full lap. Backing up 4 from your base and then moving forward enters it early. No jumping over marbles inside the safe zone; the card must fit exactly.
  • When all your marbles are safe you keep playing cards, moving your partner's marbles.
  • Deal: 5 cards each from a fresh deck, then 4 each. A second deck is used for 6 and 8 players. The shuffled order is committed with a SHA-256 hash before the first card is dealt and revealed when the deck is used up.
