How this Jackaroo plays

Teams of two, partners sit opposite. Each player has 4 marbles. Bring all 8 marbles of your team into your safe zones to win. You must play a card if any card in your hand has a legal move; otherwise you burn a card.

ATake a marble out to your base, or move 1 or 11.
KTake a marble out, or move 13 killing every marble you pass (option).
QMove 12. A red Queen may instead pick one of the next player's cards (face down) and burn it.
JBlack Jack is a swap card (⇄): swap one of your marbles with any other player's marble on the track. Red Jack: move 11.
10Move 10, or make the next player burn a card of their choice before they play.
9 8 6 3 2Move that many steps.
7Move 7, or split the 7 between two of your marbles.
5Move any marble on the track 5 steps, yours or anyone's (option). Another player's marble on its own base cannot be moved.
4Move 4 steps backwards.
JokerPlays as any card (option).
  • Landing on a marble sends it home. You cannot land on your own marble, and you cannot jump over your own marbles (option).
  • A marble on its own base (the star) is fully protected: nobody can pass it, kill it, swap it or move it with a 5, not even a King.
  • Wall: two marbles of the same team on two holes next to each other block everyone, King included, in both directions. You may land on the first one (and kill it) but not on or past the second. A pair from two different teams does not block.
  • Taking a marble out kills whatever is sitting on your base.
  • The safe zone branches off the hole just before your base, so your base is never in the way. You enter your safe zone after a full lap. Backing up 4 from your base and then moving forward enters it early. No jumping over marbles inside the safe zone; the card must fit exactly.
  • When all your marbles are safe you keep playing cards, moving your partner's marbles.
  • Each arm's holes are numbered 1–18 counting from the previous player's star, so you can count a move; hole 18 ends beside the arm's own star, and a marble enters its safe column diagonally from hole 18 of its own arm. When you pick a card, the holes you can reach light up; tap a lit hole or the marble.
  • Deal: 5 cards each from a fresh deck, then 4 each. A second deck is used for 6 and 8 players. The shuffled order is committed with a SHA-256 hash before the first card is dealt and revealed when the deck is used up.
